Privacy Policy

Effective: May 14, 2026
⚡ The Short Version

We don't collect anything. Your data lives on your device. No accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The only data that ever leaves your phone is the prompts you send to Anthropic when you use AI features — and only if you choose to.

01Who We Are

Evexia ("the app," "we," "us") is a personal fitness companion app. This privacy policy explains how Evexia handles information when you use the app on your device.

02What We Collect

Nothing. Evexia has no user accounts, no backend servers, and no analytics. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to ourselves or any third party.

Data You Create Stays on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device using your browser's localStorage:

  • Your profile (username, avatar, age, weight, sex, body fat %, experience level, training goals, equipment access, injury notes)
  • Your workout history, sets, reps, weights, and personal records
  • Your streak count, XP, rank, completed workouts, and saved programs
  • Your saved diet plans, nutrition data, and custom goals
  • Your team affiliation and community challenge progress
  • Your Anthropic API key (if you choose to enable AI features)

This data is stored on your device only. We cannot see it, access it, or recover it. If you uninstall the app or clear your browser data, this information is permanently deleted.

03Third-Party Services

Anthropic (AI Features Only)

If you enable AI features (Athlete routines, Diet plans, Nutrition calculator), the app sends your prompts directly to the Anthropic API using an API key you provide. This is the only outbound data transmission Evexia makes.

When you use AI features, the following may be sent to Anthropic:

  • Your free-text prompt (e.g., "upper body routine, dumbbells only")
  • Profile context relevant to the request (e.g., experience level, goal, equipment)

Anthropic's handling of this data is governed by their privacy policy, available at anthropic.com/legal/privacy. We do not see or store these requests.

If you never enable AI features, no data ever leaves your device.

Google Fonts

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Google may log your IP address as part of standard CDN operation. See Google's privacy policy.

04What We Don't Do

  • We do not show advertising
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or fingerprinting
  • We do not have user accounts or require sign-up
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to third parties (other than the Anthropic API calls you initiate)
  • We do not use cookies for tracking
  • We do not access your location, camera, contacts, microphone, or other device sensors

05Children's Privacy

Evexia is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Because we collect no information from anyone, this is in practice impossible — but the app is not designed or marketed for younger users, and training programs in the app are intended for adults or supervised teens. Anyone under 18 should consult a parent, guardian, or coach before starting a new exercise or diet program.

06Your Rights

Because Evexia does not collect or store your data on any server, traditional data rights (access, deletion, portability) are exercised directly by you on your device:

  • Access: Your data is visible in the app at all times
  • Deletion: Use the "Reset All Data" button in Settings, or uninstall the app, or clear your browser's local storage
  • Portability: Since data is local, you control all copies
  • Anthropic API requests: Contact Anthropic directly for any rights related to data you submit to their API

07Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the "Effective" date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's update notes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
