Privacy Policy

Effective: August 24, 2026
⚡ The Short Version

Your training data stays on your device. There are no Evexia accounts and no Evexia servers holding your workouts. We don't run ads, sell data, or track you across apps. Three things do leave your phone: purchase records handled by our billing provider, a push token if you enable notifications, and the prompts you send when you use an AI feature.

01Who We Are

Evexia ("the App," "we," "us") is a personal fitness companion app. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the App, what stays on your device, and what limited data is handled by our service providers.

This policy covers the Evexia mobile app on iOS and Android.

02What Stays on Your Device

Evexia is local-first. There are no Evexia user accounts, and we do not operate a server that stores your training data. The following is saved in your device's app storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your profile: username, avatar, age, weight, height, sex, body fat percentage, training goal, and measurements you enter
  • Workout history, sets, reps, weights, personal records, and saved programs
  • Streaks, XP, rank, completed workouts, and goal progress
  • Saved meal plans, nutrition results, and grocery lists
  • Glow Up data: habits, daily missions, supplement stack, skincare routine, and body analysis results
  • App settings such as your selected theme and expert mode preference

We cannot see, access, or recover this data. If you uninstall the App, use "Clear all local data" in Settings, or lose your device, it is permanently gone. Evexia does not currently offer cloud backup or cross-device sync.

03What Leaves Your Device

Service What is shared Why
RevenueCat A randomly generated anonymous ID, your subscription and purchase status, device platform, and app version To manage subscriptions, verify entitlements, and restore purchases across devices
Apple / Google Purchase and billing records, handled entirely by the store To process payments, renewals, and refunds
Anthropic Your prompt and the profile context relevant to that request To generate AI routines, meal plans, and nutrition analyses
Expo / push services A device push token, only if you enable notifications To deliver reminders and streak notifications

Subscriptions and purchases (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and unlock paid features. RevenueCat assigns your install a random anonymous identifier that is not linked to your name or email, and records which products you own so entitlements can be restored. We do not send RevenueCat your workout data, profile details, or health information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Payment itself is processed by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your card details.

AI features (Anthropic)

When you generate an AI routine, meal plan, or nutrition analysis, the App sends the request to the Anthropic Claude API. What is sent may include:

  • Your free-text prompt, such as a coaching style, goal, or equipment note
  • Profile context relevant to the request, which may include age bracket, weight, sex, training goal, and experience level

Because meal plans and nutrition calculations depend on body data, information you would reasonably consider health-related may be included in these requests. AI features are optional — if you never use them, nothing is sent. Anthropic's handling of this data is governed by their privacy policy. We do not store these requests or their responses on any server we control; generated plans are saved locally on your device.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, your device is issued a push token used to deliver reminders. You can revoke this at any time in Settings in the App, or in your device's system settings. Revoking permission stops notifications and the token is no longer used.

Community and leaderboards

If you join a team or appear on a leaderboard, the username, avatar, and training statistics involved may be visible to other users of the App. Choose a username that does not reveal personal information you want kept private.

Fonts and network requests

Some screens may load fonts or assets from content delivery networks, which can log your IP address as part of normal operation. See Google's privacy policy where Google Fonts is used.

04What We Don't Do

  • We do not show advertising
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or device fingerprinting
  • We do not require an Evexia account or sign-up
  • We do not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or health app data
  • We do not use your data to train AI models

05Health and Fitness Information

Evexia processes body and fitness information you enter, including weight, body fat percentage, measurements, and nutrition targets. This information is stored on your device. It is transmitted only when you choose to use an AI feature that requires it, as described in Section 3.

Evexia is not a healthcare provider and is not a covered entity under HIPAA. The App does not read from or write to Apple Health or Google Fit.

06Children's Privacy

Evexia is intended for users aged 13 and older, and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, contact us and we will address it.

Training programs and nutrition guidance in the App are intended for adults or supervised teens. Anyone under 18 should consult a parent, guardian, coach, or physician before starting a new exercise or diet program.

07Data Retention

Data stored on your device is retained until you delete it, reset it from Settings, or uninstall the App. Purchase and entitlement records held by RevenueCat and by Apple or Google are retained according to those providers' own policies and their legal and tax obligations.

08Your Rights and Choices

Because Evexia stores your training data on your device rather than on our servers, most data rights are exercised directly by you:

  • Access: Your data is visible in the App at all times, and workout history can be exported to CSV
  • Deletion: Use "Clear all local data" in Settings, or uninstall the App
  • Portability: Export your workout history from the App; all other data is local and under your control
  • Notifications: Toggle off in Settings or in your device's system settings

Deleting your data held by providers

To request deletion of the anonymous purchase record associated with your install, email support@evexia.app from the address associated with your purchase, or use the "Delete account" option in Settings, which opens a pre-addressed request. Requests are processed manually and completed within 30 days, and you will receive confirmation by email. Note that purchase and billing records held by Apple or Google cannot be deleted by us and are subject to their retention policies.

Regional rights

If you are in the EEA or UK, our lawful bases for the limited processing described above are performance of a contract for purchase and entitlement handling, and your consent for notifications and optional AI features. You may withdraw consent at any time. If you are in California, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA, and we do not discriminate against users who exercise their rights. You may contact us at the address below to exercise any applicable right.

09Security

Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security, including your passcode, biometrics, and operating system sandboxing. Network requests to our service providers use encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10International Transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries where they operate. Where required, those providers use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

11Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Effective" date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the App's update notes. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
